FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to download an app?
Nope. Your membership is a QR code delivered by text message. Show it at the wash, scan the station QR, and you're good to go.
How many washes do I get?
One wash per day, unlimited days per month. Most members wash 4–8 times per month — still way cheaper than paying per wash.
Which stations accept Car Care Club?
We're launching in Nashville with our founding partner stations. Look for the Car Care Club sign at the car wash entrance. More stations added regularly.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel before your next billing date and you won't be charged again.
I own a gas station. How do I join as a partner?
Email us at taylor@carcareclub.co or use the contact button in the "For Stations" section above. We'll reach out within 24 hours.
